# Whisker & Crime Polish Plan

## Objective

Raise the game from a strong prototype to a competition-ready multiplayer experience: faster onboarding, clearer decisions, richer feedback, stronger replay value, and a reliable final delivery.

## Pass 1: First five minutes

- Keep the first screen focused on starting a case or creating a room.
- Show player count, expected case length, and language support at a glance.
- Make the evidence board communicate progress immediately.
- Give the end screen a compact case recap and a clear replay action.

## Pass 2: Investigation feel

- Add distinct feedback for discovering, examining, and completing a clue.
- Make Barnaby and Cleo feel mechanically different within the first minute.
- Clarify multiplayer responsibilities before the room starts.
- Improve wrong-accusation feedback without interrupting the whole flow.

## Pass 3: Competitive polish

- Add a readable score and performance summary.
- Add a short replay loop with shuffled cases and meaningful variation.
- Make room state, player roles, clue ownership, and accusation readiness obvious.
- Add resilient loading, reconnect, and room-full states.

## Pass 4: Quality bar

- Verify desktop, narrow mobile, keyboard, touch, and 200% text zoom.
- Complete translations for interface, story, clues, suspects, and multiplayer messages.
- Run a two-player online smoke test from separate browsers.
- Build, validate, publish, and verify the production URL.

## Acceptance bar

- A new player understands the goal in under 20 seconds.
- A second player can join a room without outside explanation.
- Every clue discovery produces visible, readable confirmation.
- A complete case ends with a satisfying result and an obvious replay path.
- No blocking errors in build, validation, room creation, joining, accusation, reset, or mobile controls.
